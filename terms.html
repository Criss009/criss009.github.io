Terms of Use

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Scope

Master Sheet Cloud Sync is a privately operated administration tool for consolidating authorized Google Sheets provider inventories. The public pages explain its operation and privacy practices; they do not offer public access to the synchronization engine.

2. Authorized use

The operator must have permission to access every source spreadsheet used in synchronization and is responsible for complying with applicable Google, GitHub, and source-provider terms. The application is not intended to bypass sharing restrictions or access unauthorized spreadsheets.

3. Data accuracy

Provider prices, rankings, availability, and third-party website information may change or contain errors. The software attempts validation but does not guarantee that any website, listing, metric, or price remains current or correct. The operator should verify information before relying on it commercially.

4. Availability

Automation depends on Google APIs, GitHub Actions, valid authorization, and service quotas. Synchronization can be interrupted by permission changes, expired credentials, API limits, or other errors. No continuous availability guarantee is offered.
